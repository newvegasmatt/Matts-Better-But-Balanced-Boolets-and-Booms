This mod is aimed at filling in gaps in ammo and helping some weapons feel a bit better to use. This is not a hyper ammo or depleted uranium mod. Those are fun, nothing is wrong with them. But that’s not what I want, and maybe there are some that agree.

My huge thanks to Ammogen and its creator Shaneeexd. I have wanted to do something like this for awhile but do not know how to code well enough to accomplish it. Their tool allowed me to do exactly what I aimed at doing. It is also a dependency for this pack to work.

The goal of this pack is to add ammo that helps certain weapons feel more fun and useful. At first I thought I would just do the pistols and niche rounds, but ended up doing a LOT of shotgun shells. I also made some modified grenades, to spice things up. Basically, I wanted to make less loved calibers be more viable for general use. Maybe not end game or Labs run viable, but viable nonetheless.

If anyone has a suggestion, please leave a comment. My goal is balance, fun, and gameplay synergy. I will not add ridiculous ammo, unless its for an obvious meme or a terrible gun. If there is a gap you see that you think a sane and reasonable ammo would fill, let me know. Pretty much everything is either based on real life stuff, or is another ammo with “more powder”. However nothing is crazy, nothing game breaking (at least to me).

I was inspired to make this by ammo packs like Da Grogs +P, which while excellent, does break progression a good bit. I did a play through with that pack, and ended up only using the 7.62x51 DUDS rounds, because why would you not? They are hilarious. But it make things less interesting and also made it less Tarkov, if that makes any sense.

Hence, this pack is full of barely better boolets, but better nonetheless. I chose calibers that, past level 5 or 10, are pretty much useless outside of specific quests or meme loadouts. 9x18, 7.62x25, .357, you get the idea. I wanted to make some ammo that allows the weapons using those calibers to be just a little bit more viable and even, dare I say it, fun!

Balance: My goal for balance is simple, keep it as balanced as fun allows. This means that most of the ammo in this pack are in no way game breaking or changing. There are certainly better boolets in here, but some are also fun side grades like the birdshot rounds and frangible ammo. I aim for the ammo unlocks, availability, and prices to be in line with the purpose of the shell, its capabilities, and availability of similar ammos. If you think something is too strong or too weak, please leave a comment. If you also want to see something added, leave a comment. I am working on figuring out how to get custom ammos from other mods in the system so I can make new ammos from them. (Looking at you .700 Nitro, I will give you Tungsten Carbide)

Compatibility: Ill be honest and say I don’t have the time to ensure compatibility with every mod known to man. I will say I play with many of the standards like WTT armory and Ecos Emporium. Ammogen is a remarkable tool, and appears to have been able to, alongside the other mods native compatibility with custom ammo, make the ammos work right outta the box. (Buh dum tis)

Crafting: When it makes sense and is not game breaking, I have included crafts for most of the ammos in this pack. The crafts are mostly sane, with only a little bit of Tarkov-y jank in their recipes. If you see a recipe missing and think there should be one, let me know. Some of them (like the explosive ammo) probably wont get a craft and will be FiR or lightkeeper unlocks. Again, comment any suggestions you have.



Below is a list of included ammo:

7.62x25mm TT PBS gzh - 48 dam - 42 pen - Aimed at making the tokarev viable as an oh sh!t pistol. Using it in the ppsh is hilarious, but will destroy it quickly.

9x18mm PM PBP gzh - 37 dam - 37 pen - Essentially a 9x19 PBP projectile shoved into a 9x18. Slightly less powerful than the 9x19, but also has more heat and durability burn. Actually makes the makarov useful again as it can pen helmets and still has the chance to do over 35 damage.

9x21mm 7N49 UZ Zubilo - 50 dam - 43 pen - its a 7N42 with more powder. Higher heat and durability burn, so will wreck SMGs; but the Shrimp eats with it. Wanted this round to make the 5-7 maybe, possibly, not the default pistol.

9x21mm FR gzh: A leg meta special feature. High flesh damage, virtually no pen. However, the extra powder needed to propel the bullet fasted for more hydrostatic shock means more heat. A good bit more.

.366 TKM AP-ME - 86 dam - 46 pen - Take AP-M, swap SP-6 for BP GS projectile, remove some powder. Basically I wanted this to function as a less troublesome AP-M by removing much of the bogus de-buffs to accuracy recoil. .366 is already such a niche caliber, it did not need all that. The durability burn and heat still limit its “spamability.” That and the price. This should make it useful for either the 209 or bolty in .366.

.50 BMG Frangible HP - 325 dam - 27 pen - Now we are getting into the fun cartridges. This is a dup of a commercially available frangible .50 bmg round. It dumps ALL its energy inside a single 16 inch gelatin block. Its silly for flesh damage. Has a super high bleed chance, and even has fun sound effects and concussion. Obviously not the best round for .50 bmg, but certainly should be fun. Anything hit with this will be bleeding profusely.

.50 BMG MK 211 Raufoss HEIAP - 185 dam - 90 pen - Explosive - You probably thought I was referring to the SLAPP rounds earlier hehe. This is the infamous high explosive AP rounds that the US military still use to this day, war crime or not. Look up this round, its silly. In game, it has high pen but less flesh damage, however has a very small explosive. About 10 times less powerful than a RGD-5, with only a few fragments. Its enough though. A leg shot is pretty much raid ending if the recipient doesn’t outright die. Chest or stomach, good luck. Even has a small fuse that, if my math is right, should be pretty close to the real deal. Good fun. You can only find it in rare spots, and can buy like 10 at a time from Lightkeeper once you have him. So its super rare, as it should be.

.300 Blackout Punisher - 115 dam - 7 pen - Based on a real life frangible copper ammo, this nasty little devil breaks in to 3 leaves and a center base upon impact. Look up the wound cavities, definitely a war crime. But this is Tarkov! Roast those shins!

.357 Magnum Copper penetrator - 63 dam - 42 pen - Xtreme penetrator. Better pen than fmj, less damage, no fragmentation chance. Pretty simple. Can black arms at close range, but not legs. Should make the chiappa and especially the .357 deagle more appealing to use early to mid game.

.50 AE Frangible Hollowpoint - 161 dam - 9 pen - A copper tin hybrid HP designed to dump its energy as fast as possible. Lots of bleeding chance. Pretty much no armor pen. Very useful if you dump a few rounds downrange and retreat, as the target will certainly have one or two light bleeds and probably a heavy one too. Up close, dump a mag into their pelvis and they will crumple over.

.50 AE Xtreme Penetrator - 73 dam - 44 pen - Yup, here we go again. This one offers marginally better pen over FMJ, but losses some of the important 1 shot potential. Can no longer 1 tap the chest, but can black the stomach at close range. Useful to ensure helmet pens, or reliably 2 tap the thorax. And of course the butt clinching mag dumps will be quite effective.

4.6x28mm AP GX - 39 dam - 49 pen - Take a small piece of tungsten, add it to the rear to increase weight and likelihood of tumbling. Sounds like something Mechanic would do right?Oddly enough this is a downgrade over the top tier ammo. This on has less pen, but more damage. This fills a niche roll that 4.6 left open. AP SX has a hilarious pen of 53, but damage is exactly 35. Meaning any range or any damage reduction from armor pen, and you can no longer 1 tap the head. AP GX has 49 pen, and 39 damage. So it has a much longer 1 tap range. This makes for a cool synergy where the worse pen ammo gets front loaded in the mag to get reliable 1 taps, but the higher pen stuff goes after to shred the giga chads. I really like this one, let me know what you think.

4.6x30mm FRANG SX: Another leg meta gift. Basically I just made a 4.6 version of the 5.7x28mm R37 F. This is a high damage, low pen round for early game and high RoF guns.  

5.7x30mm SS90 - 52 dam - 32 pen - Your read that right, SS90. This was the original round HK intended to use. It was a super long and light round moving at stupid speed. 23 grains (1.5 grams) at 880 m/s. Silly stuff. But it just didn’t do well in penetration. I added this round as a rare find. It doesn’t really fill a niche, but i think its a cool piece or firearm history/lore.

5.7x30mm SS190 X - 42 dam - 52 pen - Put a small tungsten penetrator cap and a little extra powder behind it and you gain some AP SX pen, with AP GX damage. The downside is much higher durability burn and heating generation. So a full mag of this stuff will fold you durability and might even jam on the 1st mag. But if you top load it for a few, and then put normal 190, you get a nice combo. Expensive, and rare. Very rare.

SIG, aka 6.8x51mm. This might be a surprising one, but the base game has 2 rounds for it. 2. Even .357 gets like 5. I added three new cartridges to round out the tier 1-4 and light keeper progression. Now if you find a SPEAR at level 3 you can buy ammo for it!

6.8x51mm SIG HP - 119 dam - 17 pen - Available at peacekeeper 1, this high flesh damage low pen ammo is perfect for early game. Plus with the high ROF of the SPEAR, it can even be useful late game if your short on cash but just happen to have a spear on hand. Also its funny.

6.8x51mm SIG Brass Hunter - 95 dam - 25 pen - Available at peacekeeper level 2, this lower pen ammo allows low level players to use the spear and still do well.

6.8x51 SIG Hybrid Black Tip - 66 dam - 55 pen - OH yeah, M993 at home. This is a late game ammo, only available in raid or through lightkeeper in any reasonable quantity. Peacekeeper sells a very small quantity at a huge markup. Not much else to say.

Now the Shotgun ammo (I swear this was not my initial intention to have this many, its just fun making shotgun ammo):

Shot/pellets: 12/70 8mm Steel 0 buckshot - 42 dam - 12 pen -12 pellets - Steel 0 Buckshot for slightly better penetration. Still needs some tuning, but feels pretty good without also being busted. Definitely still struggles against armor above Tier 3 or multilayer tier 2 even. But shreds legs and the added pellets makes unprotected heads an issue.

12/70 12 Pellet 00 buckshot - 46 dam - 2 pen - 12 pellets - Pretty close clone of 8.5mm magnum buckshot, but with the more accurate 12 pellets. Similar recoil, but reduced durability burn to make mag dumps more viable. I have always had an issue with the numerous drawbacks of magnum buck in base tarkov. Modern birdshot and buckshot loads are both loaded so compactly that, in my experience, the recoil is not too different. Also, they eat the barrels pretty much the same amount as other shells. ITs only when you get into the big 3 or 3 1/2 inch shells that recoil goes crazy. My goal here was to create an alternative to magnum buck that is more fun to use. Think of this as the standard shell.

12/70 000 Buckshot - 65 dam - 1 pen - 8 pellets - What Magnum buck should have been. 8 pellets is what most 000 buckshot shells have nowadays, so only having 8 00 pellets when you’d be hard pressed to even find a non-9 pellet 00 buck is crazy to me. Whatever. This is just a bigger pellet with more damage, better range, and tighter spread. A good and dare I say it balanced alternative to the other buckshot options.

12/70 Hevi Steel BB Birdshot - 6 damage - 6 pen - 90 pellets - This was a silly idea that I actually really like how it turned out. Still working out the balancing, as nothing like it really exists in base Tarkov. Birdshot, steel birdshot. BB size (about 4 mm or so) allows on average 90 pellets to fit in a shell. Low damage per shell and terrible range, but lower recoil and devastating up close. This basically guarantees light bleeds, and often a heavy one too. The range is awful, like terrible. I made the ballistic coefficient worse than 5.25mm express by a fair margin. But the bleed chance means that even at longer ranges of up to 100m you can still pop a bleed and make the target run to fix it. If you pop around a corner and blast a few shells, you often take the target out of the fight for a good while. Sometimes they fold over 5-10 seconds later from the bleeds. Its a really unique shell that I intend to make more of and iterate on. Please try it.

12/70 TSS AP 00 Buckshot - 61 dam - 24 pen - 6 pellets - Another crazy thing you can actually buy in real life. This is a shell with 6 00 buckshot pellets made of a modern tungsten alloy. Its something like 1.8 times more dense than lead. It pretty much does not deform on impact, and was made counteract soft body armor and barriers. Goes through a windshield like it doesn’t exist. Here I made it basically anti-scav ammo. Ignores Tier 2 armor, while occasionally taking 2 shots for tier 3. The added weight of each pellet means that only 6 can fit without causing overpressure issues. From what I can tell, the numbers I have in game right now are pretty darn close to how it actually performs in real life.

Slugs: 12/70 TSS AP Wrecking Ball Slug - 172 dam - 44 pen - Loosely based on a few different real-life shot gun shells, this Tungsten alloy slug has extremely high penetration at the cost of added durability wear. In real life Tungsten has its uses, but it wears barrels and especially chokes quite quickly of you have the wrong combo. This slug, which you can actually buy, uses Tungsten to increase mass and durability of the slug itself. It essentially does not deform on impact, even with hard surfaces. Think of it as a much more rare, but slightly more penetrating AP-20. Interestingly enough, base Tarkov has a TSS AP slug in it, but only for 20 gauge. I just felt the need to complete the set for 12 gauge.

12/70 AP-21 Fragmenting AP Slug - 185 damage - 34 pen - This was just a fun little idea. Less penetrating AP-20, but with higher flesh damage and significant chance to fragment and lower bounce probability. Basically means that Tier 4 armor is more problematic, but if it hits it will do considerably more damage. Particularly more effective against legs or stomach hits. This is more of a side grade. Also causes more bleeds.

12/70 Makeshift APEx .50 BMG slug - 180 damage - 36 pen - Explosive - Remember the Raufoss round, make a makeshift .50 BMG slug with that. Enough said. Super rare in raid, another lightkeeper gatekeep. Hilarious.

KS-23:
The KS-23 shells may be the most difficult shells I have made yet. At first I went into this thinking they would be easy, Big Soviet Gun Stronk Comrade. But I quickly learned how wrong peoples perception of this gun and similar guns is. This is not a crazy soviet era 23mm super shotgun. Its a utilitarian multitool for police and specialty military units. It usually uses stun rounds, gas grenade rounds, and the flash bang. The slug is used purely to stop cars and shoot through light barricades. The shrapnel shells were almost afterthoughts. Hell, the Shrapnel 25 is pretty much a 2 ¾ inch standard 00 buckshot round adapted to fit in the 23mm shell. The system is so low pressure, it really cant push the shells at any real velocity.
Honestly, the in game rounds are too powerful for the real deal. The civilian market created much better and more effective shells. Quick note: most civilian shells were for the TOS-123, which was actually a smoothbore 26mm gun! I think they basically bored out the rifled AA 23mm barrels to make them. Most sources, of which there are few btw, estimate the civilian shells to be about on par with a 10 gauge to possible a 10 gauge magnum in power and capacity. So the three shells I added are pretty much in line with those, except I slightly reduced the velocity. Again, the KS-23 was quite low pressure, getting 1.5-2 ounce payloads to 1400 fps is gonna grenade the thing.
For reference, here is a picture f the cross sections for the rounds. https://imgur.com/rgh2Xr7 

23x75 “Shrapnel 5” Buckshot: Reload a used KS-23 shell with #4 buckshot and you have this shell. 54 pellets of #4 buck flung at barely supersonic speeds. Fun times.

23x75 Steel Birdshot: 144 pellets of BB birdshot at roughly the speed of sound is a recipe for a bad time on the receiving end. Will for sure cause a whole heap of bleeds assuming they survive the first shot. Super wide spread, very short range.

23x74mm Lead Slug: The civilian smoothbore versions rarely used slugs, but when they did it was a big ass hunk of lead launched as fast as possible. Believe it or not the Barrakada slug the military used was actually only about a .50 cal sized bullet at 500 grains. .50 BMG is a much better engine stopper. But the barakada basically can’t over penetrate, where as .50 BMG and the russian equivalent absolutely would. Thus, a 2 ounce lead slug (875 grains) being much heavier than 500, this is going to go slower. Another interesting thing, BSG coded the Barakada to be 80 grams, or over 1200 grains. That is just absurd. The Ks-23 would never handle shooting that at 420 m/s.


Grenades: Yes, I made some of those too. Good fun. Give me more ideas if you have them!

Model 4311 Super Flash Bang Grenade - Pretty much the name. Larger radius, longer lasting, but a shorter fuse so be careful! Pretty rare, but can be bought.

M67 Reduced Delay - Just like the F-1 reduced delay, the M67 version reduces the fuse time. This makes the grenade one of the best for direct, non-impact, kill grenades in the game. However, because the M67 has a larger blast radius, if you mess up the throw it is basically an instant loading screen. I included a craft to convert M67 grenades into the reduced delay version. Let me know if you want me to do the same to other grenades!

RGB-9 Enhanced Hand Grenade - A modified RGD-5, this version has a slightly shorter fuse, more fragments, and more damaging fragments. The tradeoff being that is is heavier, and thus harder to throw, and has a smaller blast radius. This is meant as a side grade to the RGD-5, and is just fun to play around with. Again you can convert the RGD-5 into this version in the workstation.
